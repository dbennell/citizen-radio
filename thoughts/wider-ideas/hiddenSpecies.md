## 🌀 **Why Keep the Hadesians Secret?**

Let's sharpen the motive: **Economic Exploitation & Cultural Danger**

* The Banu have uncovered remnants of advanced Hadesian technology or biology—perhaps bioweapons, self-replicating AI, gene-sequenced creatures, or entire intact facilities preserved by automated caretakers.

  Selling or licensing controlled access to this Hadesian technology or genetic material could vastly outweigh any trade with known civilizations, including humanity and the Xi'an.

* **Cultural Danger:**

The **Hadesian Extinction** remains deeply mysterious. You could introduce fresh speculative angles:
